## What it is

The platform behind The B!G Idea, an education programme run in secondary
schools across Ireland. Students work in teams on a project, teachers run the
class, and mentors from industry give feedback along the way. The platform is
where all of that happens.

I inherited it as a WordPress site that had been extended well past what
WordPress is for. I kept that running and built its replacement alongside it:
one application with five kinds of user, each with their own dashboard,
navigation and permissions.

## The parts I would point at

**Students do not have accounts.** A team shares a link. The platform holds far
less personal data about under-18s than a conventional login would need, and
that one decision reshaped nearly every permission check in the product.

**One design system, five audiences.** Every role gets its own colour, and every
screen still comes from the same component library. A fix to one dialog is a
fix to all of them.

**Email as a system.** Typed triggers, batching, and sends anchored to dates in
the programme calendar, with an admin view that answers "what fires when this
date moves?"

**I run it too.** Deploys, CI, staging, backups, and the box it lives on.
